At rest (before and during the action) the CRT reads as a blank dark slate, so the viewer can't see the portal holds the next world. Root cause: you call s.peek('crt') and then type the BASIC program over the screen with opaque fills. Keep the BASIC typing on the screen until the drive is bumped, then (from s.u ≈ 0.6 to the end of the action) fade the BASIC text out and fade the peek in, so by the end of the action the CRT clearly shows the 1996 home page world, bright, with a soft scanline overlay at low alpha on top and the bezel drawn after it. Also make the BASIC text brighter green with a little glow so the screen reads as a lit CRT at rest. Change nothing else.
